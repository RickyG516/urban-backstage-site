Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you build from my plans or yours?

Either. Travis has built from architect drawings and from a sketch on a napkin, and he will tell you up front which parts of a plan are going to cost you.

How many jobs do you run at once?

Few enough that you get the owner on the phone, not a project coordinator. That is the trade-off of a small builder and it is deliberate.

Are you licensed and insured in Iowa?

Registered as an Iowa contractor and insured. Ask for the certificate and it gets emailed the same day.

How long does a new house take?

Most single-family builds in Johnson County run seven to eleven months from permit to keys, weather depending. You get a schedule, not a guess.
