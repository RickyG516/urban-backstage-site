Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Who actually shows up - you or a sub?

Casey runs the crew and Renee runs the office. On residential work you are dealing with the two people whose name is on the LLC, which is the whole reason to hire a shop this size.

Are you registered with the state?

Iowa contractor registration C132075. Iowa requires it of anyone doing construction work in the state, and it is worth asking any contractor for the number before you sign anything.

When can you pour?

Iowa weather sets the calendar more than we do. Call early - spring and fall book out, and a drive that could have been poured in May often ends up waiting until September.

Do you do commercial work too?

Yes. A good share of the year is placement and finishing labor for general contractors on commercial slabs, which is also where the crew got fast.

Will it crack?

Concrete cracks. The job is deciding where - proper base prep, reinforcement and control joints put the cracks in the joints instead of across the middle of your patio. Anyone promising otherwise is selling something.
