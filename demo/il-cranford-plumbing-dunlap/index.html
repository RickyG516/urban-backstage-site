Questions we get a lot

Do you do the digging too?

Yes. Cranford Trenching & Backhoe is the same company, so a water or sewer line replacement does not need a second contractor and a second schedule.

Are you a union shop?

Yes — a UA Plumbers Local 63 signatory and a MIMCA member contractor.

How long have you been in business?

Incorporated in June 1977. Forty-nine years, BBB accredited since 2015 with an A+ rating.

Are you WBE certified?

Yes, Cranford Plumbing is a certified Women's Business Enterprise.

Residential or commercial?

Both — service and repair work for homes, and union commercial and institutional work.
