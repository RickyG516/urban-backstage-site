Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

How do I know if I need more insulation?

If upstairs rooms never keep up in winter, if you get ice dams on the eaves, or if your bills climb every year in the same house, that is usually insulation. We will get in the attic and measure what is actually there.

Blown-in or batts?

Blown-in for an existing attic or a wall cavity you cannot open. Batts for new construction, additions, or anywhere the framing is exposed. Most jobs end up with some of each.

Will it actually lower my bill?

Usually, and the attic gives the biggest return for the least money. What we will not do is promise you a dollar figure. Too much of that depends on your furnace, your windows and how you run the thermostat.

Do you do the siding too?

Yes. And if the siding is coming off anyway, that is the cheapest moment there will ever be to deal with what is behind it.

How long does an attic job take?

Most Springfield houses are half a day to a day. We put down protection, run the hose, and clean up before we go.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Family owned and operated here for thirty-four years, and happy to show the paperwork before we start.
