Questions We Get A Lot

How do I know if I need a full replacement or just a repair?

We'll get up there and tell you straight. Plenty of roofs we look at need a repair, not a replacement — we'll say so.

Do you work with insurance claims?

Yes. Storm damage claims are a big part of what we do, and we'll walk the adjuster through it with you.

How long does a typical roof take?

Most residential roofs are a one to two day job once materials are on site.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes — on both materials and our workmanship. We'll put the terms in writing before we start.
