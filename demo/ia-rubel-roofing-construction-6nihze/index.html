Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you actually do flat roofs?

Yes — flat and rubber roofs are on the BBB profile as core services. It is the work a lot of shingle-only crews turn down.

How far do you travel?

Albia, Ottumwa, Oskaloosa, Knoxville, Centerville and Chariton, and the towns in between.

Will you look at a leak without selling me a new roof?

Yes. Plenty of leaks are flashing, a boot or a seam. If a repair will hold, we will tell you that.

Do you handle insurance claims?

We document the damage and work with the adjuster. We do not promise an outcome the insurer has not agreed to.

How do I get on the schedule?

Call (641) 777-5365. Spring and after a hail event are the busy stretches, so call early.
