Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you prep the floor or just set tile?

We prep. Flattening, substrate repair and waterproofing are part of the job — skipping them is how tile cracks and grout lets go.

Can you waterproof an existing shower?

Not without opening it up. A shower that leaks needs the pan and walls rebuilt properly, and we will say so rather than tile over a problem.

Do you do countertops and the backsplash together?

Yes, and it is cheaper and cleaner that way — one crew, one layout, one set of seams that line up.

How far do you travel?

Regularly around Hamilton County and out toward Fort Dodge, Iowa Falls and Ames. Farther depends on the size of the job.
