Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you do the work yourself?

Yes. That is the whole point of the shop. The person who quotes the job is the person on site doing it.

Are small repair jobs worth calling about?

They are. A short list of small things knocked out in one visit is a normal booking, not an imposition.

How far ahead are you booked?

It moves. Call and ask, and you will get a straight answer about when the job could actually start rather than a maybe.

Do you handle the whole bathroom or just part of it?

Whole thing. Tear-out, the work behind the walls, tile, fixtures and finish, so there is one person accountable for the result.

Do you paint as well as remodel?

Yes, interior and exterior. It is usually the last step of a remodel anyway.

How long have you been in business?

Since 2014, in Freeport, under the same owner the entire time.
