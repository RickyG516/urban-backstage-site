Questions we get a lot

How long have you been in business?

Since 1954 — seventy-two years in the Milwaukee metro, formally incorporated as an LLC in 2005.

Do you do exteriors in Wisconsin weather?

Yes, and the prep is why they last. Scraping, priming and caulking properly is what separates a four-year exterior from a fifteen-year one.

Interior as well as exterior?

Both, with the same crew. Walls, trim, ceilings, cabinetry, plus decks and woodwork finishing.

Do you take commercial work?

Yes — offices, units and commercial interiors, scheduled around the business.

Will you do a single room?

Yes. Small jobs still get quoted.
