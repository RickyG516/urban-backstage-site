Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you take on residential work?

Rarely. We are set up for industrial and commercial — controls, distribution and design-build. We would rather point you at the right contractor than do a service change badly.

What does design-build actually get me?

One party responsible from the one-line to energisation. No gap between the engineer's set and what the field can actually install, and no change-order argument in the middle of it.

Can you work inside a shutdown?

That is most of what we do. Tell us the window and we will tell you honestly whether it fits or whether the scope needs splitting across two outages.

Are you a union contractor?

Yes — we are listed in the IBEW Local 34 and Local 16 contractor directories, so manpower scales to the job through the hall.
