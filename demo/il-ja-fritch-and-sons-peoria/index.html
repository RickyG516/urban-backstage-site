Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you do residential heating and cooling?

No. This shop is industrial and commercial sheet metal and HVAC. Homeowners are better served elsewhere.

Do you fabricate in house?

Yes. Ductwork and fittings are made in the Peoria shop to the job drawings, which is why field fit-up goes faster.

Are you a union contractor?

Yes &mdash; a sheet metal signatory shop, which is why we show up in the building trades contractor directories.

Will you look at an existing system?

Yes. A lot of the work here is older buildings that were right once and have drifted. Call and describe what it is doing.
