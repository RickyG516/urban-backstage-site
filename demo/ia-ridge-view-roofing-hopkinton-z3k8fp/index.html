What Ridge View Puts On Your Building

Standing Seam & Ag Panel Metal

Metal roofs built to outlast the loan on the building. Panels cut to length, hidden fasteners on standing seam, exposed-fastener ag panel where the budget calls for it.

Pole Buildings

Machine sheds, shops and storage put up start to finish — posts, trusses, sheeting and trim, with the roof detailed by the same crew that frames it.

Tear-Off & Re-Roof

Old shingles stripped to the deck so rot gets found before the new roof goes over it. Decking replaced where it needs to be, not shingled over.

Vinyl Siding

Storm & Hail Repair

Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you really work Saturdays?

Yes. Shop hours are 7am to 4pm Monday through Saturday. Roofing weather does not care what day it is, and neither does a leak.

Metal or shingles for a farm building?

Ag panel metal on outbuildings almost every time — it sheds snow, it does not lift, and it outlives asphalt by decades. Houses are more of a conversation.

Are you licensed and insured?

Iowa contractor licence C149161, active. BBB Accredited with an A+ rating since January 2025. Proof of insurance comes with the estimate.

How far do you travel?

Delaware County and the counties touching it — Manchester, Monticello, Dyersville, Anamosa and out toward Cascade. Ask about anything further; the answer is usually yes.

Can you do the building and the roof?

That is most of the work. Pole buildings go up complete — posts through trim — so one crew owns the whole structure.
