Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

My breaker keeps tripping. What does that mean?

Usually a circuit is overloaded or something on it is failing. Resetting it over and over hides the problem. Call and describe what happens when it trips.

Do I need a new panel?

Older panels are not always a problem. It depends on the age, the brand and what the house now runs. A look at it answers the question.

How fast can someone come out?

Call and ask rather than guess from a web page. You deserve a real answer about when somebody can be there.

Do you give a price first?

Ask on the call. Describe the job and what you are seeing, and you will know what to expect before anybody drives out.
