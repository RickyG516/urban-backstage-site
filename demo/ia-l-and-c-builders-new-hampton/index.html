Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

How long have you been at this?

Since 1980. Dennis Steinlage started the business and it became an LLC in 2005 — same owner throughout.

Do you only roof?

No. Roofing, siding, windows, decks, concrete, kitchens, baths and full additions. Eight trades is the point of the business.

Do you take on additions?

Yes — footings through finish. That is the work the crew is built around, not a sideline.

Will you look at storm damage?

Yes. We'll get on the roof, tell you what's actually wrong, and put it in writing for the insurance company.

How far do you travel?

Chickasaw County and the ring around it — Charles City, Waverly, Sumner, Nashua and out from there.
