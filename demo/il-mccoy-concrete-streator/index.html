Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

How long before I can drive on it?

Foot traffic in a day or two, vehicles at seven days on a standard residential mix. Justin will tell you the date, not a range.

Do you cut control joints?

Every pour. Joints are what decide where concrete cracks, and skipping them is how a driveway ends up looking random in year three.

Can you match the finish on my existing slab?

Usually. Bring him out to look at what is there — broom direction and edge detail are easier to match in person than over the phone.

Do you do stamped or coloured work?

Yes, on patios and walks. It costs more than a broom finish and he will quote both so you can see the difference.
