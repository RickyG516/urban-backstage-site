Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Are you licensed?

Yes — a Class B electrical licence with the state of Iowa, plus six additional licences on file. Ask and we'll give you the numbers.

Do you do farm work?

Yes, and a lot of it. Machine sheds, grain equipment, yard lights and outbuilding services around Calhoun County.

Why 'and Construction'?

Because a rewire means opening walls. We do the construction side too, so one outfit opens it and closes it.

Can you wire a generator in?

Yes — standby and portable hookups with a proper transfer switch, so nothing backfeeds the line.

How fast can you come out?

Call and ask. Depends on the week, but you'll get a real answer rather than a maybe.
