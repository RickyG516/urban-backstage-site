Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you take the wood away?

Yes. Brush gets chipped and the wood leaves on the truck unless you want it cut to length and stacked, which Kevin will do if you ask.

Can you get a tree out that is over my house?

That is most of the job. It comes down in pieces on ropes rather than all at once, which is slower and is the reason nothing lands on the shingles.

How fast can you come out after a storm?

Storm calls jump the list. Call the number and you will get a straight answer on the same day, not a ticket number.

Do you grind the stump too?

If you want it. Some people leave the stump, most want it ground below grade so they can seed over it. Priced separately so you can choose.
