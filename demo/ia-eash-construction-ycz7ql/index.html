What we do

What Eash Construction builds

Two things, done properly, for fifteen years running.

Metal roofing

Standing seam and exposed-fastener panel over new decking or an existing roof. Panels cut to the run so there is no seam where there does not need to be one.

Pole barns

Full post-frame builds — machine sheds, shops, horse barns, hay storage. Posts set, trusses up, sheeted and trimmed.

Re-roofs and tear-offs

Old layers come off, deck gets looked at, and the new roof goes down over something sound.

Barn repair and re-skin

Panel replacement, ridge and trim work, and closing up an old building that has started to leak.

Coatings

Roof coating work through Eash Coatings, run from the same shop on Ridge Road.

Farm and acreage work

Rural south-central Iowa is the whole service area. Gravel roads are not a problem.
