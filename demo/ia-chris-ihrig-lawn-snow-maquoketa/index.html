Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you do per-push or seasonal contracts for snow?

Both. Per-push makes sense on a short drive in a light winter. A seasonal contract makes sense if you cannot afford to wait or you need it cleared every single time. We will run the math with you instead of just quoting the bigger one.

What time do you plow?

Routes start overnight so most drives are open before people leave for work. Commercial lots get cleared before opening.

Do you salt as well as plow?

Yes, driveways, walks and lots. Worth doing on anything with foot traffic, especially if it is a business.

Can I get lawn care and snow on the same account?

That is most of our customers. One number, one bill, and we already know the property before the first snow falls.

Do you do one-time cleanups or only regular service?

Both. Spring and fall cleanups are frequently one-off, and plenty of those turn into weekly customers afterward.

How do I get on the route before winter?

Call before the first storm. Once it snows the list is full and we are running, and adding a new drive mid-storm is not fair to the people already on it.
