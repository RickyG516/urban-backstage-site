Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

How long have you been doing this?

Since 2005 out of Fort Dodge. Same owner, same town, now operating as TLC Construction & Tree Service LLC with Arbor Services as a trade name.

Do you take the wood and the brush with you?

Yes. Debris is a registered cargo class on our federal motor-carrier authority, which is a formal way of saying the mess leaves in our trucks and not onto your curb.

Can you get a tree out from over my house?

That is most of the work. It comes down in sections on ropes rather than all at once. Tell us what is under it - roof, fence, service drop, pool - before we quote it.

How fast can you come after a storm?

Faster than a one-truck outfit. Eight power units means several crews can be out at once, which is the entire reason to keep that much iron sitting in a yard in Fort Dodge.

Is the quote free?

Yes, and it is a number rather than a range. Call and we will come look at it.
