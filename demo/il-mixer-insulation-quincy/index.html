Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

How much insulation should an attic have?

In this climate you want depth, not a thin blanket. We measure what is up there now and tell you what adding to it is actually worth.

Can you insulate walls without tearing them open?

Usually yes — dense-pack goes in through small holes that get patched. We will look before promising.

Do you air seal as well?

Air sealing comes first. Insulation over leaks is money in the attic.

Do you work with builders?

Yes. We schedule to your framing and drywall dates.

Do you haul the old insulation away?

Yes. Wet, settled or rodent-damaged material comes out before anything new goes in.
