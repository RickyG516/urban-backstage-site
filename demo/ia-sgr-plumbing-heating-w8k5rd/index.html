Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you do both plumbing and HVAC?

Yes. The shop has run both since 2006, so one call covers the water and the heat instead of two contractors.

How fast can you get out for no heat?

Winter no-heat calls go to the front of the list. Call rather than send a message when the house is getting cold.

Should I repair or replace my furnace?

Depends on age and what failed. A cracked heat exchanger is a replace; most other faults are worth repairing on a unit under fifteen years.

Do you work on wells and softeners?

Yes — pressure tanks, pumps and softeners for the places outside city water.

Do you do new construction?

Full plumbing and mechanical rough-in for houses and light commercial around Jones County.

Are you licensed?

Iowa licensed and a member of PHCC of Iowa.
