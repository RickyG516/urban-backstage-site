What we do

Pumps first, and everything downstream of them

Out here the water system is the house. A pump shop that can also do the plumbing means one truck solves the whole problem instead of half of it.

Well Pump Sales

Submersible and jet pumps sized to the well and the house, not to whatever is on the shelf. Sold and installed by the same people who service them.

Pump Service & Repair

Pressure problems, short cycling, no water at all. Diagnosed at the wellhead rather than guessed at over the phone.

Pressure Tanks & Controls

Tanks, switches and control boxes replaced and set properly so the pump is not cycling itself to an early death.

Plumbing

Water lines, fixtures, water heaters and repairs. Licensed plumbing work, not a pump guy improvising.

Water Systems

Softeners, filtration and treatment for hard rural water — matched to what is actually coming out of your well.

Heating & Cooling

Furnace and A/C service alongside the plumbing side, which is why the BBB lists the shop under heating and air as well as plumbing.

Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you handle emergencies when the water is completely out?

Call and say the tap is dry. No water is not a next-week problem and it does not get treated like one.

Do you sell the pump as well as install it?

Both. The pump is sized here, sold here and serviced here, so there is no argument later about whose part failed.

Can you do the plumbing too, or just the pump?

Both. Water lines, water heaters, fixtures and treatment — the whole system, not just the thing in the ground.

How far out do you travel?

O'Brien County and the towns around it. Call with your location and you will get a straight answer, not a maybe.
