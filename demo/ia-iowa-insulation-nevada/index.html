Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you do residential attics?

The bulk of the work is mechanical and commercial — pipe, duct, boiler and building insulation on plants, schools and ag buildings. Call and ask; if it is not a fit we will say so.

Can you work during a shutdown?

Yes. Retrofit and removal work is scheduled around a plant's own calendar, including nights and weekends when that is the only window.

How do you quote?

Off drawings and specs where they exist, plus a walk-through. Linear feet, pipe size, insulation type and jacketing all move the number.

Do you make removable covers?

Yes — blanket covers for valves, flanges and fittings that have to come off for service instead of being cut out each time.
