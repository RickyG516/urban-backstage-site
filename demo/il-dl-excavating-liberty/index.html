Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you handle the sewer and water permits?

We pull what the job needs and work to whatever the county or city inspector wants to see. Tell us the address and we will tell you which jurisdiction you are in before anybody digs.

How soon can you get a machine on my place?

Depends on the season and how wet it is. Spring and late fall are the tight ones. Call and ask &mdash; we would rather give you a real date than a flattering one.

Will you be driving over my yard?

Some of it, yes &mdash; a track machine and a tandem have to get in somehow. We will walk the route with you first and tell you honestly what is going to get rutted and what we will put back.

Do you do small jobs or only big ones?

Both. A culvert, a short lateral or a stump are normal days. We would rather do a half-day job for a neighbor than have the machine sit.

What about the dirt you dig out?

If it is good fill we can usually spread it on site, which saves you money. If it has to leave, we haul it. We will tell you which one it is when we look.

Are you insured for commercial sites?

Yes, and we can send a certificate straight to your general contractor or the municipality before we mobilise.
