Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you do both the electrical and the construction?

Yes, and that is the reason to call. One shop, one schedule, and the wiring is not waiting on another trade to show up.

Are small service calls worth a trip?

They are. An outlet that quit or a circuit that keeps tripping is a normal call, not too small to book.

Can you upgrade an older panel?

Yes. It is one of the most common jobs, especially on houses whose electrical load has doubled since the panel went in.

Do you wire shops and outbuildings?

Yes. Getting real power out to a shop or shed is regular work around here, not a special request.

Do you work on new construction?

Rough-in through trim-out, and the general construction side too if that is what the job needs.

How long have you been in business?

Since 1990, out of Wadena, under the same owner for all thirty-six years.
