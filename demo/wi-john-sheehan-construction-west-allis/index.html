Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you do repairs or only full rebuilds?

Mostly repair. Tuckpointing, chimney work and stucco patching on older Milwaukee-area brick is the core of it. Full rebuilds happen when the wall has gone past repointing.

Will the new mortar match?

That is the job. Colour and joint profile get matched to the existing wall — a repoint that reads as a stripe across the facade is a repoint done wrong.

What areas do you cover?

Milwaukee County, Waukesha County and Washington County, out of West Allis.

Who pulls the permit?

John Sheehan Construction does not pull residential permits — those are pulled by subcontractors or by the homeowner. Worth sorting out before the work starts.

How long have you been at this?

Since May 1991. Thirty-five years, same trade, same three counties.
