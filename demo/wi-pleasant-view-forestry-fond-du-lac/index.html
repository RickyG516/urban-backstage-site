Depends on the season and the weather, but most jobs get quoted within a few days and done the week after. Storm damage jumps the line.

Yes. Brush gets chipped and hauled, the trunk gets cut to length, and the yard gets raked. That is the part people mention most in reviews.

Yes, and there is no obligation attached to it.
