Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you do commercial roofs or just houses?

Both. There is a commercial tear-off and replacement on the permit record in Iowa City, sixty miles from the shop. Houses are the bread and butter, but commercial is not new to us.

Are you registered with the state?

Yes - Iowa contractor registration C133608, active. Iowa requires anyone earning over $2,000 a year in construction to register with the Division of Labor. You can look it up before you call.

Can I text instead of calling?

Yes. Call or text (563) 249-8414. Texting a photo of the problem is often the fastest way to get a straight answer.

Do you do gutters too?

Yes, and skylights. It makes sense to do them while the crew and the ladders are already on site rather than booking a second trade later.

How fast can you get out after storm damage?

Call as soon as you see it. Storm work gets moved up - an open roof gets worse every time it rains.
