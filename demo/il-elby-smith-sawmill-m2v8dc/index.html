Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Can I bring my own logs to be milled?

Yes. Bring them to the yard in Stronghurst and tell us the dimensions you want out of them.

Do you only build with your own lumber?

No. The mill is there when it's the right answer - plenty of jobs use bought material.

How many trades do you actually cover?

Nineteen categories are listed on the BBB profile - remodel, concrete, siding, windows, flooring, drywall, decks, painting and cabinets among them.

Are you licensed and insured?

Insured, yes - ask on the call. Illinois doesn't issue a statewide home-improvement contractor licence.

How far do you travel from Stronghurst?

Henderson County and the towns around it - Biggsville, Media, Gladstone, La Harpe, Monmouth and out toward the river.
