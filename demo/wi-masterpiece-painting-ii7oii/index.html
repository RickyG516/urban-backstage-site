Questions We Get A Lot

Do you do interior and exterior?

Both. Exterior work is weather-dependent, so we'll schedule around the forecast.

How long does a typical house take?

Most interiors run 3-5 days, exteriors about a week depending on prep and square footage.

Do you prep, or just paint?

Prep is most of the job — scraping, sanding, caulking, priming. That's what makes it last.

What paint do you use?

Quality name-brand product. We'll tell you exactly what's going on your walls before we start.
