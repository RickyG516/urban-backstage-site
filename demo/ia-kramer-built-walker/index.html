Questions we get a lot

What is spray foam insulation?

A foam that is sprayed onto walls and ceilings, expands, and seals air leaks while it insulates.

Do you insulate pole barns?

Yes. Spray foam in pole buildings is part of the work Kramer Built shows on its page.

Do you build as well as insulate?

Yes. Custom builds, renovations, remodels and repairs are all on the service list.

Which towns do you cover?

Walker, Troy Mills, Cedar Rapids, Center Point, Urbana, Robins, Palo, Coggon and Monticello.
