Questions We Get A Lot

How often should I service my system?

Once a year for heating, once for cooling. Catching a small problem in the fall beats a no-heat call in January.

What if the heat goes out?

Call. Customers tell us we’ve squeezed them in after hours and the day after Christmas — we’ll do what we can to get you running.

How long does a system last?

Most furnaces run 15-20 years, AC units 12-15. If yours is in that range and needing repairs, it's worth pricing a replacement.

Do you only sell Bryant?

We’re a Bryant dealer for new equipment, and we service what you already have — we’ve worked through warranty headaches on other brands for customers too.
