Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you handle the insurance side of storm damage?

We document what we find and photograph it before any work starts, which is what an adjuster needs. The claim itself stays between you and your insurer.

Is it a tear-off or can you go over the old roof?

Depends what is under it. We look at the deck first — if the sheeting is soft, going over the top just hides the problem for a couple of years.

How far will you travel?

About sixty miles out of Wyoming. Stark County is home, and Peoria, Kewanee and Chillicothe are all inside that.

Who actually does the work?

David owns it and runs the crew. He is the one looking at your roof.
