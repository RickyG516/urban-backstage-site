Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

My carpet has ripples — does it need replacing?

Usually not. Ripples are nearly always a stretch problem, and power-stretching pulls them back out. Worth having it looked at before you price new carpet.

Do you have a showroom, or do you come out?

The work is mobile — the van comes to the house in Des Moines or Waukee.

Are you accredited anywhere?

Accredited with the Better Business Bureau since 2016, and A+ rated.

Can you patch pet damage in one spot?

Yes. Patching and re-seaming a damaged area is a normal job and does not mean redoing the room.

Do you take the old floor away?

Yes — removal and haul-away are part of an install.
