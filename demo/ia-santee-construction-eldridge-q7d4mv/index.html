Do you pull permits?

Every time. Seventeen permitted jobs across Davenport, Iowa City and Eldridge are on public record from 2020 through 2025, including commercial approach work.

Residential or commercial?

Both. Most of the work is residential drives, slabs and footings, with commercial approach and walk jobs mixed in.

Since 1992 — thirty-four years. Iowa contractor licence C123939, active, verified in 2026.

What is a BuildZoom score of 100?

Scott County first — Eldridge, Davenport, Bettendorf, Park View, LeClaire — and down to Iowa City when the job is worth the drive.
