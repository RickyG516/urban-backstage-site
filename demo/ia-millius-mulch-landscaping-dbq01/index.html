Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

How much mulch do I actually need?

Tell us the bed size and we will work it out. Most beds want two to three inches, and going deeper wastes your money.

Do you haul the old stuff away?

Yes. Clean-ups, trimmings and old mulch leave with us. You should not be left with a pile at the curb.

Rock or mulch — which is better?

Mulch feeds the beds and looks warmer but needs topping up. Rock costs more up front and then mostly leaves you alone. Depends how much you want to think about it.

When should bushes get trimmed?

Most things do best trimmed after they flower. If it is up against the siding or blocking a window, it gets done whenever you call.

Do you do one-off jobs or only contracts?

Either. Plenty of people call once a year for the spring clean-up and that is fine.
