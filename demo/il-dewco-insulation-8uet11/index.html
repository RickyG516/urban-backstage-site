Questions We Get A Lot

How much can I actually save?

Depends on what's there now. We'll measure your current R-value and give you a real number, not a guess.

Spray foam or blown-in?

Both have a place. Foam seals better, blown-in costs less. We'll tell you which fits your project.

Do you do existing homes or just new builds?

Both. Retrofitting an attic is one of the most common jobs we do.

Are there rebates available?

Often, yes. We'll point you to what's available in your area.
