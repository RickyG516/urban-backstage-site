What we do

What we put a brush to

Residential repaints, exteriors, and the farm and shop work nobody else wants to bid.

Interior repaints

Walls, ceilings, trim and doors. Furniture moved and covered, edges cut by hand, and the room put back the way we found it.

Exterior & siding

Wash, scrape, prime and topcoat on siding, soffit, fascia and trim. Two-coat systems on anything south- or west-facing.

Farm, shop & light commercial

Machine sheds, grain legs, shop interiors and storefronts. Coatings picked for the surface, not for whatever is on the shelf.

Cabinets & millwork

Kitchen cabinet refinishing and built-in millwork, sprayed for a factory-flat finish instead of brushed.

Drywall repair & prep

Nail pops, water stains, texture matching and patching so the new coat has something honest to sit on.

Deck & fence staining

Cleaning, brightening and stain or sealer on decks, rails, pergolas and privacy fence.

Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you carry insurance and a bond?

Yes. Iowa contractor registration C142230 is active and there is a $25,000 surety bond on file. Certificates available on request.

How far will you travel?

Roughly forty-five minutes out of Victor. Poweshiek, Iowa and Benton County work is routine; farther out depends on the size of the job.

Do you do exteriors this late in the year?

Depends on the temperature and the product. Some coatings go down fine into the forties. We will tell you straight if it should wait until spring.

Can you match an existing color?

Usually. Bring a chip, a door, or a drawer front and we can get close enough that you will not pick it out.
