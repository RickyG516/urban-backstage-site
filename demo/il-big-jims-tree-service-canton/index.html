Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you carry insurance?

Yes, and you should ask every outfit that pulls up. We are certified with the State of Illinois and with Fulton County and can show both before anybody climbs.

Will you quote off a photo?

No. A photo will not show lean, rot at the base, or what is behind the tree. Quotes are done standing under it, and they are free.

What happens to the wood?

Your choice. Cut to stove length and stacked, chipped and left for mulch, or hauled off. Say which when we quote.

Can you get in behind the house?

Usually. Twenty-seven years of tight backyards in Canton. If equipment will not fit we climb and rope it out.

How fast after a storm?

Hangers and anything over a roof or a drive move to the front of the list. Call the number, do not wait on a form.
