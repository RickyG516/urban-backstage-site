Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

How long before I can park on a new driveway?

Foot traffic the next day. Drive on it at seven days in good weather, longer if it turns cold. We will give you the real date on the day we pour, not a stock answer.

Can you match the colour of my existing patio?

Usually close, rarely exact — concrete cures to its own shade and an older slab has weathered. We bring samples and tell you honestly how near we can get before you commit.

Do you haul the old concrete away?

Yes. We are a registered carrier and run our own trucks, so tear-out and haul-off are part of the job rather than a subcontractor you have to chase.

Do you do the fireplace on a stamped patio yourself?

Yes — that is the point of doing it in one pour. The footing and the slab go in together so there is no seam between the patio and the fire feature.
