Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Are you the same people as Ziegler Electrical?

Effectively, yes. Blaze Electric bought Ziegler Electrical Services and kept the shop, the employees and the phone number. The name on the invoice changed.

Do you do farm work?

Yes — barns, shops, grain handling and yard lighting. Agricultural wiring has its own rules and we work in it regularly.

Can you handle the Focus on Energy incentive paperwork?

Yes. We're a registered Trade Ally for business lighting, so the rebate side is handled as part of the job instead of left to you.

How far will you drive?

Seven counties: Rusk, Chippewa, Taylor, Price, Sawyer, Washburn and Barron. Outside that, ask — it depends on the job.

My panel is full. What are my options?

Usually a service upgrade or a sub-panel. We'll look at the load and tell you which one actually fits instead of selling you the bigger one by default.

How fast can you get out?

Depends on the week and where you are. Call (715) 532-0112 and we'll give you a real day instead of a maybe.
