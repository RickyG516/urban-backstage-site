Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you do flat roofs or just shingles?

Flat and low-slope is the specialty — TPO single-ply, heat-welded. Shingle work gets done too, but the flat roof experience is what forty years bought.

Can you find a leak another roofer missed?

That is a large part of the work. On a Cedar Rapids commercial addition after the derecho, the building leaked from day one and the original contractor could not find it. Primmer was brought in and found it.

Are you licensed and bonded in Iowa?

Iowa contractor registration C138098, active, with a $25,000 bond. Both are public record and worth checking before you hire anyone.

How far do you travel?

Walker is the base. Cedar Rapids, Marion, Vinton, Center Point and the towns around them are routine.

Do you handle insurance claims?

Storm, wind and hail damage gets documented for the carrier. Bring the adjuster into the conversation early.
