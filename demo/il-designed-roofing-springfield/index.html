What we do

What Designed Roofing puts on a building

Commercial-first. Tear-off, re-roof, and the flashing and metal that decides whether it leaks.

Ballasted & adhered EPDM

Single-ply membrane roofs on schools, museums, halls and warehouses. Tear the old system off, deck it, and put the new one down in courses.

Roof replacement & tear-off

Full removal of the failed system before anything new goes down. No layering over a wet deck.

Waterproofing & tuckpointing

Coping joints, parapets and wall details — the places a flat roof actually leaks.

Roof sheet metal
