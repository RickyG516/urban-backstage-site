Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Are you union?

Yes. Work is performed under the Illinois Prevailing Wage Act at published rates — $93.00 an hour basic and $96.00 foreman on straight time under the current state agreement.

How much of the interior can you self-perform?

Framing, drywall and taping, EIFS and stucco, plaster, insulation, acoustical and specialty ceilings, fireproofing and flooring. That is most of an interior package under one contract.

Do you work on public buildings?

Yes. The Illinois State Treasurer holds a sole-source agreement with Landgrebe for contracting and general facility repair at the Marine Bank Building on One East Old State Capitol Plaza.

How long have you been doing this?

Since 1988 out of Springfield. Mike Landgrebe is president; the office carries a dedicated project manager and estimator.

Will you do a small tenant build-out?

Yes. Call with the square footage and the schedule and the office will tell you straight whether it is a fit.
