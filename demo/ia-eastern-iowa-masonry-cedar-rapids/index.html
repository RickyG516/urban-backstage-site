Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you only do brick, or the waterproofing too?

Both. Flashing systems, caulking and building-envelope waterproofing are part of the scope, which is what property managers usually actually need.

Will the new mortar match?

That is the job. Colour and joint profile get matched to the existing wall — a repoint that reads as a stripe is a repoint done wrong.

Residential or commercial?

Both. Commercial envelope work and residential brick and stone.

Do you do new construction?

Yes — new masonry as well as restoration and repair.

What areas do you cover?

Cedar Rapids and Linn County out across eastern Iowa, including the Iowa City corridor.
