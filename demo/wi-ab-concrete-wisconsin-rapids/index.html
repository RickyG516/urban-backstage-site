Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

What does the city list mean?

Wisconsin Rapids publishes the concrete contractors approved to work in the city. We are on the 2026 list, which is a matter of public record and worth checking on anybody you hire.

How thick should a driveway be?

Four inches over compacted base for cars, five to six if a loaded truck or a camper will sit on it. We will say which yours needs and why.

Can a cracked slab be saved?

Sometimes. A settled panel can be lifted or replaced on its own; a spalled surface that is losing aggregate is usually past patching. We will tell you which before quoting.

Do you work for general contractors?

Yes. Pads, curb, walks and equipment bases as a sub, and we are listed on The Blue Book ProView for exactly that.

When is it too cold to pour?

Later than most people think, with blankets and mix adjustments. There is a point where we will tell you to wait rather than take your money.
