Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you do the whole project or just part of it?

The whole thing — grading, drainage, hardscape, planting, lighting and sod are all in-house, along with the tree and stump work.

When should a retaining wall get built?

Anytime the ground is workable, which in Wausau is roughly April through November. Booking earlier in the season gets you a better spot on the calendar.

Do you take commercial snow contracts?

Yes — commercial snow removal and salting are part of the year-round side of the business, not a favour done for landscape clients.

Can you fix a yard that holds water?

That is a drainage and grading problem, and it is on the service list. It usually gets solved before any hardscape goes on top of it.

Do you maintain what you build?

Yes. Lawn maintenance, bed care, spring and fall cleanups, and pond upkeep are all available on the same account.
