What we do

Four kinds of electrical work, one crew

Farm service is its own trade. So is a panel swap in a 1920s house. Hoddy Gates has been doing both out of the same shop since the Nixon administration.

Farm & Ag Electrical

Yard poles, grain leg and dryer feeds, shop subpanels, well and pump circuits, livestock building wiring. Three-phase where you have it, and a plan where you don't.

Service & Panel Upgrades

Moving off a 100-amp fuse box, adding capacity for a shop or a heat pump, replacing a panel that has been recalled. Permit and inspection handled.

Commercial & Industrial

Machine feeds, lighting retrofits, controls, motor circuits and plant maintenance. Listed as a commercial electrical subcontractor with the Blue Book network.

Residential Wiring

New circuits, kitchen and bath remodels, ceiling fans and recessed lighting, outdoor and garage power, EV chargers, whole-house surge.

Generator & Backup Power

Interlocks, manual and automatic transfer switches, and standby sets sized to what you actually need to keep running when the line goes down.

Troubleshooting & Repair

A breaker that will not stay in, a dead circuit, flickering lights, a burned receptacle. Diagnosis first, then a price, then the fix.

Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you still do farm work?

Yes — it is a core part of the business, not a sideline. Yard poles, dryer and leg feeds, shop panels, livestock buildings, pumps and wells.

Can you pull the permit?

Yes. Permit and inspection coordination is part of the job on anything that needs it, including service changes.

How far out do you travel?

Regularly across Linn County and into Jones. Farther for commercial and industrial work — ask when you call.

My panel is old. Does it all have to go?

Not always. Sometimes it is a breaker or a feeder. You get a look at it and a straight answer before anybody quotes a full service change.

Is there emergency service?

Call the shop number and describe what is happening. Genuine no-power and burning-smell calls get moved to the front.
