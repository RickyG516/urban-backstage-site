Questions We Get A Lot

What flooring holds up best with pets and kids?

Luxury vinyl plank, usually. We'll walk you through the tradeoffs on each material.

Do you move the furniture?

Yes, that's part of the job. We'll talk through anything oversized ahead of time.

How long does an install take?

Most rooms are a one to two day job. Full-house runs about a week.

Do you remove the old flooring?

Yes — tear-out and disposal are included in the quote.
