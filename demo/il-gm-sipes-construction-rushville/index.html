Questions we get a lot

Do you work for IDOT?

Yes — G.M. Sipes is a named contractor on Illinois Department of Transportation awards for concrete and asphalt patching.

Do you handle your own traffic control?

Yes. Barricades and traffic control are run in-house rather than subcontracted, which is the difference on a live roadway.

Five power units and six drivers under USDOT 2015080, active, with no reportable crashes.

How far do you travel?

Rushville is the base and the highway work runs statewide.

Do you take commercial site work?

Yes — flatwork and structural concrete alongside the highway work.
