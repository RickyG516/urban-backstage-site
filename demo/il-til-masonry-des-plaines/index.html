What we do

Restoration work, not patchwork

Most of what we do is the structural end of masonry — the parts a building fails on, not the parts that just look tired.

Tuckpointing & repointing

Grinding out failed joints and repointing to match the original mortar profile and colour. Whole elevations or spot repair.

Lintel replacement

Rusted steel lintels cut out and replaced, brick rebuilt over the opening. The $126,700 job on N Leavitt was exactly this, at scale.

Parapet rebuilds

Rebuilding parapet walls and coping that have gone soft at the top of the building, before water gets into the roof deck.

Chimney repair & rebuild

Repointing, crown repair, or taking a chimney down to the roofline and rebuilding it true.

Masonry restoration

Brick and stone replacement, cleaning, sealing, and matching old material on buildings that have to keep their face.

Commercial & multifamily

Restaurants, medical offices, retail build-outs and apartment buildings — permitted work with a licensed mason contractor of record.

Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Are you licensed to do permitted work in the city?

Yes. We hold City of Chicago Mason Contractor licence Class B, number MC5994, and we are the mason contractor of record on permitted jobs.

Do you do small jobs or only buildings?

Both. The permit record runs from a few thousand dollars of spot repointing up to six-figure commercial restoration.

Will the new mortar match?

That is the job. We match joint profile and mortar colour to the existing wall — a repoint that reads as a stripe across the elevation is a failed repoint.

How soon can you look at it?

Usually within the week for a look and a number. Open violations and GC deadlines get moved up — tell us on the call.

Do you work with general contractors?

Regularly. Restaurant build-outs, medical offices, retail and multifamily are a normal part of the mix.
