Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you do masonry and concrete, or just one?

Both. The BBB record lists mason contracting and eight separate concrete categories, which is unusual for a shop this size.

Can a bowed foundation wall be repaired?

Often, yes. It depends on how far it has moved and why. We will look before quoting rather than guessing over the phone.

Do you repoint old brick?

Yes. Matching mortar and joint profile on older brick is a large part of the work around here.

What is concrete sawing for?

Cutting an opening for a doorway, a trench or a partial replacement without breaking out and repouring the whole slab.

Who will I be dealing with?

Thomas or Tammy. There is no sales rep — the owners quote the work and the owners do it.
