Dubuque and the Tri-State Area

New View works within roughly a 30-mile radius of Dubuque, IA — across the Iowa, Illinois and Wisconsin state lines.

Dubuque, IA

New View Landscaping is based in Dubuque, so it's not a drive for us, it's home. Most jobs on the books are Dubuque properties, from bluff-side lots overlooking the Mississippi to new-build subdivisions on the west side.

View service area →

Asbury, IA

Asbury is the second-largest city in Dubuque County and sits right on Dubuque's west edge, most of it built out in subdivisions over the last few decades.

View service area →

Peosta, IA

Peosta is one of the fastest-growing cities in Iowa right now, its population has more than tripled since 2000, and most of that growth is brand-new construction.

View service area →

Galena, IL

Galena sits in the Driftless Area, the one part of the Midwest the glaciers never flattened, so the terrain is real hills and limestone bluffs instead of the flat ground most of Iowa has.

View service area →

Platteville, WI

Platteville is a college town in Wisconsin's Driftless Region, home to UW-Platteville, with the same rolling, hilly terrain as Galena but across the state line to the north.

View service area →
