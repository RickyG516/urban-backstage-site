Landscape Lighting

Path lights, uplighting and accent lighting that show off the work after the sun goes down.

A landscape you spent real money on should still look like something after dark. New View installs low-voltage path lighting, uplighting on trees and structure, and accent lighting on patios and walls.

Newest addition to the lineup — usually bundled into a full design and install project, but available as its own add-on for an existing yard.

  • ✓ Low-voltage path and step lighting
  • ✓ Uplighting for trees, walls and structure
  • ✓ Patio and fire pit accent lighting
  • ✓ Works as an add-on to an existing landscape
