Landscape Lighting

Path lights, uplighting and accent lighting that show off the work after the sun goes down.

A landscape you spent real money on should still look like something after dark. New View installs low-voltage path lighting, uplighting on trees and structure, and accent lighting on patios and walls.

Newest addition to the lineup — usually bundled into a full design and install project, but available as its own add-on for an existing yard.

Everything we install runs on low-voltage LED fixtures, which means the running cost is genuinely low, we're talking cents a night for a typical whole-yard setup, not a noticeable bump on the electric bill. LED fixtures also run cooler and last far longer than old-style halogen landscape lighting, so it's less maintenance over the life of the system, not more.

The best time to add lighting is during the original hardscape or planting install, since wiring can go in before beds are mulched and pavers are set. That said, lighting is one of the easier things to retrofit onto an existing landscape afterward, it doesn't require redoing the wall or patio it's highlighting. Most systems can be run on a simple timer or a smart controller if you want it handled automatically at dusk.

  • ✓ Uplighting for trees, walls and structure
  • ✓ Patio and fire pit accent lighting

Common Questions

Landscape Lighting FAQs

Does landscape lighting add much to my electric bill?

Barely anything. Low-voltage LED fixtures run on cents a night for a typical whole-yard setup. It's not a system that shows up as a real cost on your bill.

Can lighting be added to a yard that's already landscaped?

Yes, it's one of the more straightforward retrofits. It doesn't require redoing the wall, patio or beds it's meant to highlight.

Do the lights turn on and off automatically?

Most systems run on a simple timer or a smart controller set to dusk, so you're not manually flipping a switch every evening.
