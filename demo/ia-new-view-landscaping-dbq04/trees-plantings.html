Trees & Plantings

Trees, shrubs and perennials picked for the spot, not just what looked good at the nursery.

A plant that's wrong for the spot is a callback waiting to happen. New View picks trees, shrubs and perennials based on the actual sun, soil and space on your property, not what's on sale that week.

Planted at the right depth with the right backfill, staked where wind exposure calls for it, and placed as part of the overall design instead of scattered in afterward.

  • ✓ Trees, shrubs and perennials matched to the site
  • ✓ Proper planting depth and backfill
  • ✓ Staking where exposure requires it
  • ✓ Placement planned as part of the full layout
