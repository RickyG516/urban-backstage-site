Landscaping in Peosta, IA

Peosta is one of the fastest-growing cities in Iowa right now, its population has more than tripled since 2000, and most of that growth is brand-new construction.

Peosta has exploded over the last two decades, new subdivisions, a growing industrial park, and Northeast Iowa Community College's main campus all pulling in development. What that means for us: a lot of Peosta calls are brand-new builds with a blank-slate yard, no existing landscaping to work around, just bare dirt and a grading plan. That's design-and-install territory, sod and grading to get the lot usable, then beds and hardscape built around it.

About about 10 miles from our Dubuque shop.
