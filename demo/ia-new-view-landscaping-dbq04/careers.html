Work With New View

New View Landscaping is a growing, owner-operated crew based in Dubuque, IA. If you want real hands-on work and a crew that actually shows up for each other, apply below.

Why Work Here

What it's like on the crew

Owner-Operated

You work alongside Dallas, not under a rotating cast of foremen. He's on the job with the crew, not just running the business from an office.

Real Skills, Fast

Design, hardscape, grading, planting, lighting — the work is varied enough that you actually learn the trade, not just the same task on repeat.

Growing Company

New View is picking up steady work across Dubuque and the Tri-State area. Growth means real opportunity for the right people to take on more.

Local, Not Corporate

No call center, no franchise playbook. Straight talk, fair pay, and work that's actually in your own community.

What We Look For

No résumé required to start the conversation.

No formal landscaping background needed — we'll teach the trade. What matters more:

  • ✓ Shows up on time, every time
  • ✓ Willing to learn and take direction on-site
  • ✓ Comfortable with physical, outdoor work in all seasons
  • ✓ Valid driver's license (a plus, not always required)
  • ✓ Takes pride in doing a job right the first time

Don't see an opening that matches? Apply anyway. We're growing, and we'd rather hear from good people before we need them than after.
