About New View Landscaping

Owner-operated by Dallas Rolwes, built on referrals and work that holds up.

New View Landscaping is a Dubuque-based design and install company run by Dallas Rolwes. Dallas walks every property himself, builds the plan, and is on site for the install — not handing the job off to a crew you've never met.

The work covers full landscape design and installation, retaining walls, patios and walkways, bed and mulch work, sod and drainage, tree and shrub planting, and landscape lighting — across roughly a 30-mile radius of Dubuque.

Most of the business comes from referrals. That means the work has to hold up after the truck leaves, not just look good in a photo the day it's finished.
