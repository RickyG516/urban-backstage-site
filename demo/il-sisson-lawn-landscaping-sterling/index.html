Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you do design and the installation, or just one?

Both. The design is drawn here and the same company installs it, so there is nobody to blame but us.

Can you add irrigation to a lawn that is already in?

Yes. It means pulling pipe rather than trenching open ground, and we will tell you honestly what the lawn will look like afterwards.

Do you handle paving as well as planting?

Yes - paving contractor is one of our listed trades. Patios, walks, drives and steps.
