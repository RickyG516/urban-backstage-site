Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

How long have you been in Anamosa?

Since June 1981. Same business, same family, same shop at 803 N Division Ct.

Do you do septic as well as plumbing?

Yes. We are on the Iowa CIOWTS certified installer roster, so we can put in and replace septic systems, not just plumb to them.

Are you licensed for both plumbing and heating?

Both. Iowa plumbing and Iowa mechanical. That is why we can take a job from the water main to the furnace without bringing in a second outfit.

Who answers the phone?

Nancy or Mike. There is no call centre and no dispatcher.

Do you work outside Anamosa?

Yes — Jones County and the towns around it. Call and ask; if it is too far we will say so.
