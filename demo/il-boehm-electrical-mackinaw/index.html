Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you pull the permit, or do I?

We pull it. Every job on the public record above was permitted under our name, and the inspection is our problem to schedule and pass, not yours.

Will you do a single outlet, or is there a minimum?

Small residential work is fine. Call with what you need and we will tell you straight whether it is worth a trip charge or whether it should wait and get bundled with something else.

Can you handle a commercial job on a tight schedule?

That is most of what the permit log is. Restaurant and retail remodels run on somebody else's opening date, and we plan the rough-in and trim around it.

Do you work on new construction or only existing buildings?

Both. Multi-family new construction - underground conduit, unit rough-in, garages, site lighting - is a regular part of the year.

How do I get a number out of you?

Call and describe the job. For anything bigger than a service call we come look at it before quoting, because guessing a price over the phone is how people get surprised.
