Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

How soon can I drive on a new slab?

Walk on it in a day or two. Keep vehicles off a fresh driveway for a week, and longer if we poured it in cold weather. Concrete keeps gaining strength for a month after it looks finished.

Broom, stamped or exposed aggregate?

Broom is the workhorse and the cheapest, and it is what most driveways get. Stamped costs more and reads like brick or stone. Exposed aggregate hides wear and tear the best. We go through it before anything gets ordered.

Do you take the old concrete out?

Yes, and removal and haul-off are in the quote. It is not a line that shows up at the end after the work is done.

Why did my last driveway crack?

Almost always base prep or missing control joints. Concrete moves no matter who pours it. The job is to decide where it cracks, not to pretend it will not.

When in the year can you pour?

Spring through late fall is the easy window in Dubuque. Cold weather pours need blankets, more time and a bigger allowance for weather. Call early, the calendar fills by summer.
