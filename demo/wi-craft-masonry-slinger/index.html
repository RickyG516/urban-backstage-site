What we do

Seven things we do

All of it under one name, which is unusual for masonry this far out of Milwaukee.

Brick masonry

New brick, additions, chimneys and repair. Matching an existing brick and mortar colour is most of the skill on a repair.

Stone masonry

Natural and cut stone — walls, veneer, piers and caps. Laid, not glued on.

Custom masonry

The one-off work: a pizza oven, a stone mailbox, a retaining wall that has to hold a grade.

Fireplace construction

Firebox, throat, flue and hearth, built so it drafts instead of smoking into the room.

Colored and stamped concrete

Integral colour and stamped pattern, done in the pour rather than painted on after.

Concrete flatwork

Drives, walks, patios and slabs — poured by the same crew that does the masonry.

Excavation

Our own machine. Dig, backfill and grade without waiting on somebody else's schedule.

Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

How long has Craft Masonry been around?

Since 1967 — fifty-nine years, out of Slinger the whole time.

Do you do repair work or only new?

Both. Chimney and brick repair is steady work, and matching the existing brick and mortar is the part that takes the experience.

Do you do the concrete too, or just the masonry?

Both. Flatwork, colored and stamped concrete, and the masonry. Plus we run our own excavator, so the dig is ours as well.

Can you build a fireplace that actually drafts?

Yes. Firebox, throat and flue sized together is what makes the difference between a fireplace and a smoke problem.

Do you pull permits?

We will tell you straight: the BBB file notes we do not pull permits, so that piece stays with the homeowner or the general. Ask and we will walk you through what your village wants.
