Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

How fast can you get out for no heat in January?

Same day for existing customers in the Lakes, and we will tell you straight if it will be tomorrow instead of guessing to keep you on the phone.

Do I have to buy Trane?

No. We are a Trane dealer because the parts support is good, but we install and service other equipment and we will say when a repair beats a replacement.

Do you handle seasonal and second homes?

Yes — a lot of our work is lake property. Shut-down, start-up and winter checks on a house nobody is living in are routine here.

Who actually shows up at the door?

One of our own technicians. We run a state-registered apprenticeship program and train in-house rather than subbing the call out.
