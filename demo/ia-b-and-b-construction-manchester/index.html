What we do

What we do

Roofing. Not roofing plus six other things.

Roof replacement

Tear-off, deck inspection, underlayment and new shingles. Rotten decking gets replaced, not shingled over — which is the part that decides whether the roof lasts.

Roof repair

Leaks, wind damage, missing shingles, failed flashing. Repairs are worth doing when the roof has life left, and we will tell you honestly when it does not.

Storm and hail damage

Eastern Iowa gets hail. We will go up, document what is actually damaged, and give you something you can hand to your adjuster.

Flashing and ventilation

Chimneys, valleys, pipe boots and ridge vent. Most leaks we get called about are flashing, not shingles.
