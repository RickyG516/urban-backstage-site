What we do

Repair and new build, not one or the other

The BBB file lists eleven categories for May Masonry — chimney repair and foundation repair at one end, prefabricated masonry panels and commercial general contracting at the other. Most masons pick a lane.

Chimney repair & rebuilds

Spalling brick, a failed crown, mortar you can pull out with a thumbnail. Repointed or rebuilt from the roofline up, depending on what the inspection actually shows.

Foundation & structural masonry

Foundation repair, block work and the load-bearing masonry underneath the rest of the house. Twenty-four years of Iowa freeze-thaw is the relevant experience here.

New construction, residential & commercial

Full new masonry including prefabricated panel work — carried under both Residential and Commercial General Contractor listings, which is the part that puts this on commercial sites.
