Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you do residential?

The crew is set up for commercial and light-industrial work — larger elevations, occupied buildings, scheduled phases. Larger or specialty residential stucco and EIFS work is worth a call.

What is Lotusan?

A silicone-resin exterior finish engineered to shed dirt and water instead of holding it. On an Iowa exterior that means fewer washes and a longer stretch between recoats than conventional paint.

Can you fix failing EIFS?

Yes. Most EIFS failures start at windows, sills and penetrations rather than in the field of the wall, so the repair is as much detail work as it is finish work.

Do you handle the drywall too?

Yes — hang, tape, finish and texture match. Doing the substrate and the finish with one crew is the whole point.

How far do you travel?

Cedar Rapids and the corridor as a matter of course. Further out for multi-building or repeat work.

How do I get a number?

Call and get a walk-through booked. Commercial finish work does not price accurately off a photo.
