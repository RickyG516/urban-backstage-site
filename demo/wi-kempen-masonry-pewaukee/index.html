Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you do small repairs or only full walls?

Both. A cracked chimney crown or twenty feet of repointing is a normal week here, same as a new garage wall.

How do I know if my chimney needs repointing or rebuilding?

If the mortar can be raked out with a key and the brick above the roofline is loose, it is usually a rebuild. We will tell you which one it is before quoting.

Is the business licensed and insured?

Yes - Wisconsin Building Contractor registration 1123686, and we carry liability and workers compensation.
