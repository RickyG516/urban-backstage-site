Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

How far will you travel for a job?

Fifty miles from the office at 4631 Lost Trail in Vesper. That covers Wood County and reaches into Portage, Clark and Marathon.

Do you do agricultural work?

Yes. Barn pads, feed bunks, shop floors and machine sheds are a regular part of the schedule.

What is an insulated concrete form wall?

The forms stay in place and become the insulation. You get the structural wall and the R-value in one pass instead of framing and insulating afterwards.

Can you fix a slab instead of replacing it?

Often. Settled slabs can be lifted and spalled surfaces patched. If the concrete is past that, we will say so rather than sell you a patch that fails next winter.

How soon can you get out to look?

Call the office at (715) 435-3553. Scheduling is tighter from May through October, so earlier is better.
