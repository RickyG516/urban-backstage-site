Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you sell the material or do I buy it?

Either way. We can supply it, or lay what you have already bought. Labour-only is fine and we will tell you up front if the material is going to give trouble.

Can my hardwood be saved instead of replaced?

Often yes. If there is enough thickness left to sand, a refinish costs a fraction of a tear-out and the boards are usually better than anything new.

How long does a room take?

Most single rooms are a day. A whole main floor is two to three. Tile runs longer because the set has to cure before anyone walks it.

Do you move the furniture?

We will shift what is reasonable. Anything heavy or fragile is better handled before we arrive, and we will say so when we measure.
