Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

How do I know if I need a whole roof or just a repair?

Age, what the decking looks like once a course is lifted, and how many places are failing at once. We will tell you if a repair genuinely buys you a few more years &mdash; that is a cheaper job for you and an honest one for us.

What happens if you find rotted wood under the shingles?

It gets replaced, and you hear about it before it does. It is the most common surprise on a tear-off and it is why a quote that does not mention decking is not really a quote.

Can you do a metal roof on a house, not just a shed?

Yes. Standing seam on a house lives or dies on the edge and valley detailing, which is the part worth asking any roofer about before you hire them.

Will my yard and landscaping survive it?

Tear-off is messy by nature. We tarp, we magnet-sweep for nails, and we will walk it with you at the end. If something got damaged we would rather you point at it than find it later.

Do you work with insurance after a storm?

We will get up there, document what we find and give you something you can hand to an adjuster. We are not going to tell you a claim will be approved &mdash; that is the adjuster&rsquo;s call, not ours.

Is there a drive charge if I am outside Shawano?

Ask when you call. For most of northeast Wisconsin it is just part of the job; a long haul we will be upfront about before we quote.
