Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Can you save a tree instead of removing it?

Often, yes. This crew holds a pesticide-application licence and licensed-arborist status, so disease and pest problems can be diagnosed and treated rather than going straight to removal.

Do you work near power lines and buildings?

Yes. Sectional rigging and lowering is the standard method here, which is what makes tight removals possible without dropping anything.

Are you licensed and insured?

Do you do commercial properties?
