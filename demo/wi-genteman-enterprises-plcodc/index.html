Questions we get a lot

Do you pull the building permit?

No — permits are pulled by the homeowner or the sub-contractor on residential work. Justin will tell you exactly what is needed before anything starts.

Do you do small repairs or only full remodels?

Both. A door that will not shut is as welcome as a kitchen gut.

How far do you travel from Granton?

Clark County is home ground, and Marshfield, Neillsville and Loyal are routine. Ask about anything farther.

Is Genteman Construction LLC the same business?

Yes. Genteman Construction LLC is the registered name; Genteman Enterprises is what is on the truck.
