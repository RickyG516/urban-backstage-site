Questions We Get A Lot

What finishes do you offer?

Broom, smooth trowel, exposed aggregate, and stamped. We'll show you samples before you pick.

How long before I can drive on new concrete?

Foot traffic in about 24-48 hours, vehicles in about a week. We'll give you the exact timeline for your pour.

Can you pour in cold weather?

Yes, with the right mix and blankets. We'll tell you honestly if the forecast means we should wait.

Do you remove the old slab?

Yes — tear-out and haul-off are part of the quote.
