Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you do both the electrical and the data wiring?

Yes — that is the point of having both crews. Power, network cabling and alarm wiring get pulled on the same visit instead of across three contractors and three schedules.

Can you trench the service run yourself?

We own the trencher. New shop, shed, grain setup or addition — the underground run and the panel work are the same job to us.

Is this still the same Dean's Light Box?

Same company, same name. Dean started it in 1977; his son Jeff runs it now.

Do you take commercial and farm work?

Both, alongside residential. A good share of the work is commercial and agricultural wiring around Bremer County.
