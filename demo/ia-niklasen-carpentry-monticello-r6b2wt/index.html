Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Are you in Monticello or Farley?

Both, in a sense — the shop address is on 190th Ave outside Monticello and the mailing address runs through Farley. Same phone, same Dean, one business.

Do you take small jobs?

Yes. A door that will not latch and a full addition both get a call back. Sixteen categories on the licence for a reason.

How long have you been doing this?

Since August 2000. Incorporated in 2004, BBB Accredited since 2012 with an A+ rating the whole time.

Do you sub the work out?

Very little. Framing, siding, roofing, decks and finish carpentry are all in-house, which is why the framing and the trim actually line up.

What area do you cover?

Jones County and the ring around it — Monticello, Anamosa, Cascade, Dyersville, Farley, Olin, Wyoming and Onslow.
