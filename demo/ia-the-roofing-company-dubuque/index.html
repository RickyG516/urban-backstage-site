Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

How fast can you get out after a storm?

Same day or next day for tarping if water is actively coming in. Full inspections usually inside 48 hours. After a big hail event the whole tri-state calls at once, so the sooner you are on the list the better.

Will you work with my insurance company?

Yes. We document the damage the way adjusters want to see it and we meet them at your house. What we will not do is tell you to file a claim we do not think will hold up.

How long does a full replacement take?

Most Dubuque houses are one day, start to finish, including cleanup. Steeper roofs, or ones where we find rotten decking, can run into a second day. We tell you which one you have before we start.

Do you tear off the old shingles or lay over them?

We tear off. Going over the top hides everything wrong underneath and adds weight your rafters were not designed for. Code allows a second layer in some cases. We still do not recommend it.

What if it rains in the middle of the job?

We only tear off what we can dry in the same day, and anything open gets covered before we leave. Watching the radar is part of the job around here.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, and we will hand you the certificate before anyone climbs a ladder. If a contractor gets cagey when you ask that, that is your answer.
