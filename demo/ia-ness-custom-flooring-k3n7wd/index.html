Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

What flooring is best for pets and kids?

LVP, laminate and tile are great for durability and easy cleaning. Avoid soft woods or light-coloured carpet for high-traffic homes.

What is the most durable type of flooring?

Tile and SPC vinyl are extremely durable. Hardwood can be durable but is prone to scratching.

What flooring is waterproof or water-resistant?

Luxury Vinyl Plank, tile and some laminates are waterproof or water-resistant. Hardwood is not waterproof and can warp.

Can I install new flooring over old flooring?

Often yes, especially with floating floors like LVP or laminate, if the subfloor is level and clean. We check the manufacturer specs before we commit.

Which flooring is easiest to clean and maintain?

LVP, tile and laminate need minimal maintenance - sweeping and mopping. Carpet needs regular vacuuming and periodic deep cleaning.

Do you do custom showers?

Yes. Full custom tile shower builds including pan, waterproofing, niche and bench.
