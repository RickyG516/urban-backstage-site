Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Can this be repaired instead of replaced?

Usually, and we will say so. Most calls here are edge, flashing and valley problems, not a worn-out field of shingles.

Do you handle ice dam damage?

Yes. It is the common Dells failure &mdash; water backs up under the lower courses and shows up inside months later. Gutters and drainage are part of the fix, not an upsell.

Can you work around rental bookings?

Yes. A lot of the work here is cabins and seasonal rentals, so scheduling around booked weekends is normal.
