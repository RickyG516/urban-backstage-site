Questions

Permanent Lights, answered.

Do permanent lights have to come down each season?

No. Permanent lights are installed once along your roofline and stay up all year. They are designed to blend into the trim during the day.

Can I change the colors and patterns?

Yes. Colors, patterns and schedules are controlled from your phone, so you can switch from warm white to holiday colors in seconds. Try the light studio on this page to preview the idea.

Are permanent lights worth it in an Iowa winter?

Permanent lighting is built to stay up through the season, and Dubuque’s long, dark winters are when a well-lit home stands out most. We will walk through durability and what to expect during your estimate.

Do you install permanent lights outside Dubuque?

Yes. We serve Dubuque and communities within roughly 30 miles, including parts of Iowa, Illinois and Wisconsin. See our service areas page.
