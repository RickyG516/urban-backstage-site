Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

How long before I can drive on it?

Foot traffic in a day or two, vehicles closer to a week. We will give you the real number for your pour and the weather that week.

Can you pour in cold weather?

Yes, with the right mix and blankets. We will also tell you straight when the forecast means waiting is the smarter call.

Do you take the old slab out?

Yes. Tear-out and haul-off are part of the quote, not a surprise line at the end.

What finishes can I actually pick from?

Broom, smooth trowel, exposed aggregate and stamped. You will see samples before anything gets ordered.

Do you work in Illinois and Wisconsin too?

Yes — East Dubuque, Kieler, Hazel Green and Galena are all normal weeks for us.
