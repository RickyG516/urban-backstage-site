After the pour

When can I use it?

The question every customer asks before the truck pulls away. Here is the honest version. Cold weather stretches all of it, and we will tell you by how much.

24hDay one

Stay off it

Forms may still be on. It looks hard and it is not. A footprint at this stage is permanent.

48hDay two

Walk on it

Foot traffic is fine. Keep pets off, keep furniture off, and do not drag anything across it.

7dWeek one

Drive on it

Cars and light trucks. Loaded trucks and dumpsters wait longer, and we will say so if that is what is coming.

28dFull strength

Seal it

Concrete keeps gaining strength for a month. Sealing before it has finished curing traps moisture and does more harm than good.

Straight answers

Before you call

Will it crack?

Every slab moves. That is why we cut control joints, so it cracks on a line we chose instead of across the middle of your patio. Joints placed right are the difference between a slab that ages and one that fails.

Can you pour in cold weather?

Yes, with the right mix and blankets. We will also tell you straight when the forecast means waiting a week is smarter. We would rather pour it late than pour it twice.

Do you take the old slab out?

Yes. Tear-out and haul-off are in the quote, not a surprise line at the end of the job.

What finishes can I pick from?

Broom, smooth trowel, exposed aggregate and stamped. You see real samples on site before anything is ordered.

Do you work in Illinois and Wisconsin?

Every week. East Dubuque, Galena, Kieler and Hazel Green are normal stops, not special trips.
