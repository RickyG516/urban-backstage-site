Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Is ice guard really necessary here?

On a Jackson County eave, yes. Snow melts over the warm part of the roof, refreezes at the cold overhang and backs water up under the shingles. Membrane is what stops that reaching the ceiling.

Why metal in the valleys?

A valley carries water off two planes at once. Shingle-woven valleys wear through at the centre line, and that leak shows up inside before it shows up outside.

Can you do an insurance job?

Yes. The roof gets walked and photographed and the write-up matches what is up there, which is what makes an adjuster conversation short.

Do you replace bad decking?

Whatever is soft comes out once the old roof is off. You see it before it is covered up.
