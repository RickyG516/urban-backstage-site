What we do

Inside, outside, and the prep nobody photographs

AE Painting is listed under Residential Painter, Painting Contractors, Commercial Painter, Exterior House Painting and Pressure Washing. That last one is the reason the rest lasts.

Interior painting

Walls, trim, ceilings, cabinets. Cut lines that hold up when you look at them in the morning light instead of under a work lamp.

Exterior painting

Siding, trim and soffit on Wisconsin houses, which means working around a short season and knowing when it is too cold or too wet to put coating on.

Pressure washing & prep

Chalked siding, green north walls, a deck that has not been touched in six years. Washed and dried before a brush comes out — paint over dirt is a one-season job.

Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you do commercial as well as houses?

Yes — AE Painting carries the Commercial Painter category alongside Residential Painter and Exterior House Painting.

Is pressure washing a separate job or part of painting?

It can be either. It is listed as its own service, and on most exteriors it is the step that decides whether the coating holds.
