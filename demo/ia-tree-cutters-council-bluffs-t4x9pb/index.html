Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We come look at the tree. Trying to price a removal off a phone description is how people get surprised on the invoice.

What happens to the wood and brush?

It goes with us unless you want it. Brush gets chipped, wood gets hauled. We rake the ruts before we pull out.

Can you take a tree down over my house?

That's most of what we do. It comes down in sections on a rope instead of getting felled, so nothing lands on the roof.

Do you grind the stump too?

We can. It's priced separately from the removal — say so when you call and it'll be on the same quote.

Do you work in Nebraska?

Yes. Douglas and Sarpy County are inside our normal range along with the western Iowa counties.

How do I pay?

Cash or check at the moment. Ask when we quote it so there's no surprise at the end.
