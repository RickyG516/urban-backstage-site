Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you do residential plumbing, or only commercial?

Our bread and butter is commercial, industrial and institutional. Call and describe the job — if it's not a fit we'll tell you straight and point you somewhere it is.

Do you fabricate your own duct?

Yes. Sheet metal is fabricated in our own Keokuk shop. That's why a field change doesn't turn into a two-week lead time.

Are you a union shop?

Yes. We're a signatory contractor with Sheet Metal Workers Local 91 and a member of the Mechanical Contractors Association of Iowa.

Can you handle a plant shutdown window?

That's a lot of what we do. Give us the window and the scope and we'll tell you honestly whether it fits in it.

Do you work in Illinois?

Yes — Hamilton, Warsaw and the surrounding Hancock County area are inside our normal working range.

How do I get a price?

Call the shop at (319) 524-1566 or send the details through the form. Drawings help; a phone description is fine to start.
