Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you haul the old concrete away?

Yes, on our own trucks. Tear-out, load and haul are part of the quote, not a separate contractor you have to chase.

How long before I can drive on it?

Foot traffic is usually a day or so, vehicles longer. Don gives you the actual window for your pour and the weather that week.

Can you just cut concrete without pouring?

Yes. Saw cuts for doorways, trenches, patch sections and control joints are a job on their own.

Why did my last slab crack?

Almost always the base or the joints. Both get looked at before we quote a replacement, or you get the same result twice.
