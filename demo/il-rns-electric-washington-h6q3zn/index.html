Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Do you do residential work?

Most of the work is commercial and industrial: restaurant and retail buildouts, service upgrades, industrial power and controls. Residential service calls get done too. Meter replacements and moves, coordinated with Ameren, come up regularly.

Are you union?

Yes — IBEW Local 34 electricians, NECA signatory. Manned for prevailing-wage and commercial work.

How far do you travel?

Washington is the base. Peoria, Bloomington-Normal, Champaign and Jacksonville are all regular ground.

Can you hit an opening date?

That is most of what we do. Buildouts get scheduled backwards from the open, with inspections built into the plan.

Do you handle sign and low-voltage circuits?

Yes, including sign installation circuits, telecom and alarm rough-in.

How do we get a bid?

Send the prints and the scope. Call the office and we will tell you what we need to price it.
