What we do

Four jobs, start to finished ground

Most outfits drop the tree and leave you a stump. JLM covers removal, pruning, stump removal and stump grinding — all four, because the stump is part of the job.

Tree removal

Dead, split, leaning over the garage, or just too close to the house. Taken down in sections where there is no room to drop it whole.

Tree pruning

Deadwood out, weight off the limbs that overhang the roof, and shaping that lets a healthy tree keep growing instead of tearing itself apart in the next storm.

Stump removal & grinding

Ground out below grade so you can mow straight over it, not a two-foot reminder left in the middle of the lawn.

Questions we get a lot

Do you take the stump out too?

Yes — stump removal and stump grinding are both part of what JLM does. You should not have to hire a second outfit to finish the first one's job.

Who actually shows up?

Josh Moore and his crew. Josh does the estimate himself and works the job alongside the guys.

Do you take emergency calls?

Yes — 24-hour emergency service for a tree that is down or hanging over something. Call (618) 317-1044.

Will you prune instead of removing it?

If the tree is worth keeping, yes. Pruning, deadwood removal and taking weight off limbs over the roof are all on the list — removal is not automatically the answer.
