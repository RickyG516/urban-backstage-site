Questions we get a lot

Are you a union shop?

Yes — an IUPAT member and a signatory in the Painters District Council 58 directory.

Do you still do veneer plaster?

Yes. Veneer plastering and traditional plaster are still on the crew list, which is increasingly rare.

Commercial or residential?

Both, though the commercial work spans seven CSI divisions — gypsum board, plaster, EIFS, wall finishes and more.

Do you do EIFS?

Yes — exterior insulation and finish systems, installed and detailed properly.

How long have you been in business?

Since 2010, BBB A+ with the file open since 2012.
