Questions We Get A Lot

Do I need a panel upgrade?

If you've got a fuse box, breakers that trip often, or you're adding a big load like an EV charger, probably yes.

Do you install EV chargers?

Yes — we'll check your panel capacity first and tell you what it actually needs.

Do you handle emergency calls?

Yes. Anything involving burning smells, sparking, or a dead panel gets treated as urgent.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, fully licensed and insured. We pull permits on everything that needs one.
