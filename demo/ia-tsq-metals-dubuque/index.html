Capabilities

Everything a part needs, under one roof.

Parts don’t leave the building between processes. Cut, formed, welded, coated and shipped from the same shop, so there’s one lead time and one phone number.

CAP-01 / LASER

Laser Cutting

Four Bystronic lasers up to 8 kW fiber, with automatic loading. Carbon steel to 1″, stainless and aluminum to 5/8″.

CAP-02 / FORM

CNC Bending

SafanDarley E-Brake: 176 US tons across a 120″ bed, with graphical bend simulation before the first hit.

Specs →
CAP-03 / WELD

Robotic Welding

Three robotic cells on two-station turntables: one side loads while the other welds. Manual welding for everything else.

Specs →
CAP-04 / FINISH

Powder Coating

In-house line with a 3-stage wash and a 3′ × 4′ × 12′ capacity. Any colour. John Deere paint-certified.

Why TSQ

You send the print. We own it from there.

  • Prototype to production. One-offs and test cuts through contracted production runs, programmed so every quantity runs cost-effectively.
  • Engineering on staff. Fixture design, R&D and experimental parts, not just cutting what’s on the page.
  • Measured, not eyeballed. FARO Edge measurement arm, calibration-certified, for CAD-to-part checks.
  • Room to run your job. A 44,000 sq ft plant, the first to open in the Dubuque Industrial Center South, plus a 31,350 sq ft addition announced in 2022.
  • Delivered. We deliver anywhere in the tri-state area.
