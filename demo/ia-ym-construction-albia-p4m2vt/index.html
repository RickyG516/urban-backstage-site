Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

How big can you build?

Standard post-frame runs from a small machine shed up to a full shop or riding arena. Tell us the span and the height you need and we will tell you what the framing takes.

Do you do the concrete too?

Yes. Slabs get poured inside the posts so the building finishes on a real floor rather than dirt.

Standing-seam or exposed-fastener?

Both. Standing-seam hides the fasteners and lasts longer; exposed-fastener costs less up front. We will price it either way.

My old metal roof is leaking at the screws.

That is usually gaskets, not the panel. Re-screwing with oversized fasteners often buys years before a re-roof is worth it.

How far do you travel?

Albia is the base. Ottumwa, Oskaloosa, Centerville and Chariton are routine; farther out depends on the job size.

Are you insured?

Yes, and BBB accredited since October 2018.
