What we do

Exclusion, not a spray can

There is no pesticide for bats in Wisconsin, and there should not be. The only thing that works is finding every gap and closing it in the right order.

Full Building Inspection

Soffit lines, ridge vents, gable ends, chimney flashing, fascia gaps, dormer returns. Every opening found and mapped before anything gets sealed.

Humane Exclusion

One-way devices on the active entries so the colony leaves and cannot get back. Timed around Wisconsin's maternity season so no pups are sealed in.

Sealing & Exclusion Work

Permanent closure of the entries and the gaps that would become entries — mesh, flashing, sealant and trim, matched to the building.

Attic Guano Cleanup

Removal of accumulated droppings, treatment of the affected area, and insulation assessment where the deposit has gone through it.

Commercial Buildings

Apartment blocks, churches, schools, barns and warehouses. Larger envelopes, more entries, the same method and a written scope.

Follow-Up & Verification

A return visit to confirm the colony is out and the work held. If something is still moving, that gets dealt with.

Straight answers

Questions we get a lot

Can you just spray or trap them?

No, and nobody should. Wisconsin bats are protected and there is no legal pesticide for them. Exclusion — letting them out and sealing the building — is the method that works.

Is there a time of year you cannot do it?

Yes. During maternity season the pups cannot fly, so sealing the entries would trap them inside. The inspection and the sealing plan happen then; the exclusion is timed around it.

Another company already treated it and they came back.

That is a common way people find us. Usually the active entry was missed or the sealing was partial. The inspection starts from scratch.

Do you handle the mess in the attic?

Yes — guano removal, treatment of the area, and a look at whether the insulation underneath is salvageable.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes. Apartments, churches, schools, barns and warehouses, alongside houses.
