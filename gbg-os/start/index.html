Logging in

First time on a device

  1. Go to urbanbackstage.com/gbg-os (no www).
  2. Type your email. Cloudflare emails you a code. Type the code.
  3. On the orange GBG screen pick your name and enter your PIN. Ricky sends PINs by text, never in a doc.
  4. When your phone or browser asks to save the password, tap Save. Your PIN fills in on its own next time.
  5. That device stays logged in. Use Sign out on the Hub if it is a shared device.

If it won't let you in

  • Make sure it says urbanbackstage.com, not www.
  • No code? Check junk mail. Codes come from Cloudflare.
  • Wrong PIN wait is a couple seconds. Try again or text Ricky.

Tasks

Everything with an owner and a date. Tap the box to finish it (Undo is there if you misclick). Tap the words to change the date, owner or add notes like who you talked to. Search at the top.

Jobs

Every lead from the phone line and the website lands here. Tap a card to see the customer, add notes, set a follow up, move it along. Marking a job Done creates the closeout task on its own.

Contacts

Customers, partners and vendors. Search, open anyone, archive or delete. The EXAMPLE entries show how a filled out record looks.

Roles

Rate every role on how good you'd be and how bad you want it. Nobody sees anyone's scores until all three finish. Think we missed a role? Use the suggestion box at the bottom.

Gear

Check off the tools you already own. Add quotes. The cards show which service is cheapest to start.
