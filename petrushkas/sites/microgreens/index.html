How a tray grows.

Same five steps, every tray, every week.

Sow

Seed spread even across a clean tray.

Blackout

Covered in the dark while roots take hold.

Light

Under the lights to green up and fill out.

Harvest

Cut at the base, weighed, and logged.

Deliver

Packed cold and dropped at your door.
