00 Your Day 1 Checklist

Do these in order. Don't skip ahead. By the end you're ready to dial.

Goal

Be making real calls by the end of today. Not tomorrow. Today.

Before your first dial

New reps must pass the Academy gauntlet — 10 of 12 — before their first live dial session. No exceptions.

  1. Open the Sales OS. Go to urbanbackstage.com/sales-ops. Bookmark it. That's your home base — it opens the Workspace, the single screen that holds every cockpit and tool. (The old link hub still lives at /sales-ops/hub/ if you ever want it.)
  2. Bookmark your personal link. Bookmark this URL with your own rep ID: urbanbackstage.com/sales-ops/cold-call/?rep=your-id — it auto-selects you every time, on any device, no dropdown needed.
  3. Set your daily goal. The ⌘ K button top-right opens the goal modal. Set it to 10. That's your target today.
  4. Read The Opener section below. Section 06. Know the three styles — Word for Word, Guided, Freestyle. Pick one to start with. Word for Word if you're new.
  5. Open Cold Call cockpit. Cockpits → Cold Call. Read the prospect card. Know what Quick Win, Hook, and Ask For mean.
  6. Click Load Dials. 10 contacts pull from HubSpot. Your queue is live.
  7. Browse before dialing. Hit Preview All 10 Dials. Look at who's in the queue. Check trades. Know what you're walking into.
  8. Open Trade Info in the toolbar. Right side, 🏗 Trade Info. See what auto-selects on the first contact. That's your cheat sheet.
  9. Dial #1. Click DM Free when they answer. Follow the script. Log the outcome. The system does the rest.
  10. After 10 dials: check Commission Tracker. Tools → Commission Tracker. This is where your earnings live.
Important

Always set the rep selector before loading dials. Your HubSpot data, your queue, your commission — all tied to your rep ID. Wrong rep = wrong data.

01 Why this exists

Most sales tools are built for enterprise teams with Salesforce budgets. This is built for two people trying to sign contractor clients faster than any agency around them. No tab switching. No copying and pasting scripts. No logging calls manually in a spreadsheet. One screen. Everything you need.

The contractor you're about to call is getting pitched by agencies every week. Most reps stumble through the call with no prep. This system gives you trade intel, a proven opener, objection handlers, and HubSpot logging — all in real time while you're on the phone.

  1. Open urbanbackstage.com/sales-ops in Chrome on your desktop or phone.

Every deal you close must have UNC Revenue Type set before it appears in your commission tracker. Go to the deal in HubSpot → Deal properties → UNC Revenue Type. Options: Monthly Retainer Y1, Monthly Retainer Y2+, One-Time Project Build, Branded Merchandise.

04 Daily morning routine

  1. Check Callback Queue first. Tools → Callback Queue. Overdue callbacks at the top. Call those before new dials.
  2. Open Cold Call cockpit. Set goal. Load dials.
  3. Preview queue. 👁 Preview All N Dials. Scan trades, cities, any familiar names.
  4. Dial session: 15 calls, break, 10 calls. That's the cap. Don't try to grind 50 in a row — quality drops hard after 15.
  5. Log every call. Every single one. Even no-answers. The data compounds over time.
  6. After session: check Activity dashboard. Today's dials, HOT+WARM rate, outcome breakdown.

05 Running a dial session

The prospect card

Before you dial, read the card. Takes 10 seconds. While the phone rings you have:

  • Quick Win (green) — the most likely gap for their trade. Mention it if they ask why you called.
  • Hook (orange, italic) — the sharpest line for their trade. Click it to copy. Read it if they ask "what do you do?"
  • Ask For (blue) — who answers at this type of business. Know before you ask.
  • Best call window (yellow) — when this trade is most reachable. Don't ignore this.
  • Last outcome — if they're WARM, this is a callback not a cold call. Treat it differently.

Branch buttons

When they pick up, immediately click or press the keyboard shortcut:

ButtonKeyMeaning
DM Free1Owner answered, free to talk. Deliver the opener.
DM Busy2Owner answered but mid-something. Get a real callback time.
Gatekeeper3Someone else answered. Identify type, get a window.
Wrong Number4Auto-logs, instant next contact.
Use the keyboard

Never touch the mouse mid-call. Press 1, 2, 3, or 4. Then press the next number as the screen advances. Your eyes stay on the script, your hands stay on the keyboard.

No answer / voicemail

Don't answer → use the Voicemail tool (📱 in the toolbar) to grab a script, leave the VM, log No Answer. Move on immediately. Never sit on a no-answer.

06 The UNC opener

One opener. Three ways to deliver it depending on your confidence level that day.

The philosophy

Declare the cold call immediately. Ask one qualifying question. Shut up. If they want to grow, you're in. If they don't, move on. Your time is worth more than a hard sell on someone who doesn't want it.

Word for Word W

"Hey [first name] — [your name] from Urban Niche Co., this is a cold call. I help contractors get more jobs online and keep the phone ringing. Quick question — are you trying to grow the business right now, or are you good where you're at?"

Read it exactly. Every word is there. Use this when you're new or on an off day.

Guided G

Four anchors shown on screen. Hit each one in your own words: declare cold call → your name and Urban Niche Co. → I help contractors get more jobs online → are you trying to grow? Use this once you have 50+ calls under you.

Freestyle F

Goal only: find out if they want to grow. Yes = go. No = next dial. Use this when you have the opener fully internalized and want to sound completely natural.

After they answer YES

Hit branch 1. The screen shows a trade-specific pitch. Read the hook line in the toolbar (🏗 Trade Info). Then offer the audit: "I run a free audit on your online presence and send you a 90-second video of exactly what's costing you calls. No pitch. Want me to send it over?"

Objections

Hit branch 2 from the pitch screen. Pick the objection. Say the script. Come back to the close. Don't fight objections — redirect them.

Inbound

For form fills and inbound calls. Speed-to-lead flow — qualify fast, book the call, don't let hot leads go cold.

Proposal Walkthrough

6-section proposal walk. Progress dots. Section-level objection handler — pick the objection, handle it, return to where you were.

Referral Ask

For happy clients. Direct referral ask + cross-vertical pivot if no names come up immediately.

Renewal / Upsell

Month-3+ client calls. Health check, wins recap, upsell trigger, intervention if churn risk is present.

Win-Back

Lost client re-engage. No pitch — diagnose the real reason they left, then earn round 2 or get the intel and move on.

Re-Engage

Warm handoff calls for prospects who already received the email sequence. Phone → permission → email only. Never try to close on this call — goal is to get permission to send a personalized follow-up.

Onboarding Kickoff

New client first call. 5-section interactive checklist: access, brand, assets, month-1 success criteria, comms rhythm. Buyers remorse save script included.

11 Your commission

Commission is earned when UNC receives cleared payment.

Unpaid invoices don't trigger commission. Partial payments trigger commission on the amount received only.

Revenue TypeRateNotes
Monthly Retainer — Year 130%Months 1–12 per client. Of each monthly invoice paid.
Monthly Retainer — Year 2+10%Month 13+ per client. Residual, paid monthly while client is active.
One-Time Project Build25%Websites, AI builds, consulting setups.
Branded Merchandise50%50% of UNC net profit on the deal.

Key rules

  • Clawback: If a client cancels or doesn't pay within 60 days of their first invoice, first-month commission is deducted from your next payment.
  • Residuals: Year 2+ (10%) continue as long as the client is active AND you're under this agreement. They stop on termination.
  • Payment timing: Paid within 15 days of UNC receiving cleared payment. Monthly statement by the 10th.
  • Claude reimbursement: $100/mo covered by UNC when you have 1+ active retainer client. Business expense, not commission.
  • Set UNC Revenue Type: Every deal you close in HubSpot must have this property set or it won't appear in your Commission Tracker correctly.

13 Rules of the road

  • Declare the cold call. Always. First thing out of your mouth.
  • Ask one qualifying question, then shut up. The first person to talk after a good question loses.
  • Log every call. No answer, voicemail, wrong number — all of it. The data is what makes the dashboards useful.
  • Don't fight objections — redirect. "Totally fair. Let me ask you this..." disarms 90% of pushback.
  • One close per call. Audit offer OR the deal. Pick one and drive toward it. Never double-ask.
  • If they don't want to grow, move on immediately. Your time is worth more than a hard sell on someone who doesn't want it.
  • Set UNC Revenue Type on every closed deal. No exceptions. Commission Tracker won't work without it.
  • Callbacks before new dials. Always. A WARM callback is worth 5 cold dials.
  • Read the Quick Win before every dial. 10 seconds. Makes you sound like you did homework.
