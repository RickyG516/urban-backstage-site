The Golden Rules
🤫
Silence is your friend. After you say something good, shut up. Let it land. The first person to talk loses.
🎯
Never argue. Redirect. You're not trying to win an argument. You're trying to get them to a decision. Every answer moves toward yes or a respectful no.
↩️
Acknowledge and redirect. "Totally fair. Let me ask you this..." disarms 90% of pushback. Don't fight objections — redirect them.
☝️
One ask per call. Either the audit call or the deal. Don't double-ask. Pick your target and drive toward it.
