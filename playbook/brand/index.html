Voice

Direct. Real. No fluff. Contractors can smell fake. Plain English always. Write the way you'd talk to a guy on a job site who's busy and has heard three pitches this month. Short sentences. Concrete numbers. No hedging.

Content Strategy

Private operator content. Facebook and Instagram business pages only. Static graphics. No face, no video. Three posts a week, Monday / Wednesday / Friday. Scheduled through Buffer — Meta Business Suite is legacy.

Four pillars, rotate them:

Contractor EducationTeach something they can use today
Industry InsightsWhat's changing in their trade or market
Service ExplainersPlain-English what we do and why it works
Quiet ProofResults, no chest-beating

The goal is legitimacy and sales support — not audience growth. Quiet money and privacy. We're not building a personal brand.

Proof Points

Fuehrer Painting LLC — qualified lead within 24 hours of site launch.
